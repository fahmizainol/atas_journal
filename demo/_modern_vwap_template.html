Modern VWAP [GBB] — on our tape

A port of the published Pine (data/research/modern-vwap/modern_vwap_gbb.pine), run over cached NQ sessions on bars. Four constructions: a swing anchor that re-anchors at every confirmed pivot instead of at a fixed time, KER-adaptive bands, a two-axis regime read, and two regime-gated signals. Switch the anchor and watch what the line does at a pivot — that is the part of this indicator we have neither built nor falsified. The split view puts the plain session VWAP we already read beside it, on the same bars.

Not a backtest. No exit rule, no sample worth a statistic. The table reports how far price travelled in the __FWD_MIN__ minutes after each signal — distances, not results. The author's own six-year test across BTC/ETH/SOL came back at zero, and he says so in the video.

Anchor

Session / week / swing. A swing anchor resets the accumulator at each confirmed pivot — strict on both sides, confirmed pivot bars later, then backfilled from the pivot bar. Our VWAP work has only ever used clock anchors.

How this is put together
  • Data — the existing tick cache only (data/cache/ticks/*.parquet). Nothing is fetched, so the page costs nothing to rebuild. Bars are ET bars built from trade prints at , and they exist only where ticks do — a hole in the tape closes up rather than becoming an empty bar, which is what the Pine sees too. Every window in the indicator counts bars, not minutes.
  • The bar size is not a resample. Each timeframe is a full rebuild: KER(__KER_LEN__), the __REGIME_LEN__-bar medians and the pivot lengths are all bar counts, so they cover different amounts of clock at each size and the regime, the anchors and the signals genuinely differ. Only the onside/offside window is held constant in time (__FWD_MIN__ min), so those two columns mean the same thing on every timeframe.
  • Warm-up — the regime medians need __REGIME_LEN__ bars, so the indicator runs across the three cached days behind each session and only the session is drawn. Context days must be the same contract: a roll inside the window would put a price gap through the middle of every trailing median. Where fewer same-contract days are on disk, the regime reads undefined (grey) until its medians fill.
  • Anchors — swing5/10/20 are his swing anchor at three pivot lengths (at the current bar size, swing10 wants of confirmation each side). globex is the CME trading day (18:00 ET), which is what timeframe.change("D") resolves to on a futures feed and so the closest thing to his "session"; rth is our own 09:30 NY anchor, there for comparison. Month is dropped — a month anchor over a five-day window is one flat line.
  • The split view — the same bars, the same candles, with a plain globex-anchored VWAP and fixed 1/2/3σ bands: no swing re-anchoring, no KER scaling, no regime colour. The two charts share a time axis and a crosshair, so a signal on the left can be read against where price actually sat on the session VWAP at that moment. Its price scale is its own — the bands differ, so the two autoscale slightly differently.
  • The line steps, it does not repaint. A swing confirms pivot bars after the fact and the accumulator is backfilled from the pivot bar, so the line jumps at the confirmation bar. Bars already drawn keep the old anchor's values. What a live trader sees is a step, not a rewritten past — the video's "redraws backwards once" is not what this code does.
  • The gate is recorded, not applied. Every raw trigger is kept; blocked means the regime it fired in was not the one it belongs to. You cannot see what a gate is doing by looking only at what survived it, so the switch above shows both.
  • Onside / offside — the furthest price got each way within (__FWD_MIN__ min) of the next bar's open, which is his backtest's entry and the only honest one for a close-confirmed signal. No target, no exit rule, no stop: these are distances.
  • Two things to watch for. MR is our touch-bar close-back shape verbatim — the one the weekly-band context study resolved as an artifact of scoring the touch bar's own close. And a band scaled by a vol proxy is the shape the ATR × upper-band study resolved as "intraday ATR is the band renamed". Neither is a reason not to look; both are reasons not to build off a first impression.
