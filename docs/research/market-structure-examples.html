Chop & structure — eight example trades

Run cdc07ca2 (v10 baseline), 1-minute candles, times ET. Each panel spans ~110 minutes before entry through exit. The yellow wash marks the overlap_10 window — the last 10 completed 1-min bars before entry whose bar-to-bar range overlap defines the chop score. The violet line is the causal 10-pt zigzag — swings as they were confirmed at entry time (no hindsight).

1 · Chop — bar overlap before entry

Top row: two stops entered out of rotation — the ten bars in the wash sit on top of each other (overlap 0.71–0.74), no impulse behind the pullback. Bottom row: two winners entered out of marching tape (overlap ≈ 0.47) — bars make progress, the pullback has a trend to lean on.

2 · Structure — trend clarity, not direction

Top row: two stops taken in mixed structure — the zigzag's last swings disagree (one higher extreme, one lower); note the tape itself is not choppy (overlap ≈ 0.50) — this failure mode is independent of section 1. Bottom row: confirmed structure pays in both directions — a clear-uptrend +3R and a clear-downtrend +3R (a V-day band bounce).

Companion to docs/research/market-structure-winloss.md. overlap_10: mean intersection of consecutive 1-min bar ranges ÷ their average range over the 10 bars before entry. zigzag trend state: +1 = higher high & higher low, −1 = lower high & lower low, 0 = mixed.
