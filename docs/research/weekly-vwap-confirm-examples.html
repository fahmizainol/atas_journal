Weekly-band fade — wait-for-confirmation, worked examples

Real events from confirm_test.py, featured rule two_close (two consecutive 1-min closes back on the mid’s side of the touched weekly ±1σ; 30-min window; abort if price first trades 0.30σ through the level). Orange = weekly mid/±1σ/±2σ; purple dashed = touch; green = confirmation entry; dotted gray = the ±0.30σ race around the entry price; red dashed = the abort level over the wait window. Won/lost/abort samples are deterministic (earliest + latest); the cost-of-waiting pair is explicitly value-selected to illustrate the give-up.

The cost of waiting

The two confirmed events with the largest immediate-minus-confirmed edge deficit (explicitly selected to show the mechanism): by the time the second close prints, most of the rejection has been paid for. This give-up (−0.17σ mean, paired) is why confirmation nets out to nothing.
