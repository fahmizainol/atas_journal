EMA reclaims at scale: the tail, the type, the mode — and the churn

A "reclaim" = price closes above an EMA after spending ≥45 of the prior 60 minutes below it. The joint 50+200 single-push reclaim (n≈580 over 363 sessions) produces spectacular tails — but its median sits exactly on the any-minute baseline, its modal outcome is a whipsaw back below within 15 minutes (75%), and at matched distance from the 200 a fresh cross drifts the same as an old one. Five real events below, picked at the distribution's landmarks — not its highlights.
