The opening stack: what the 20/50/200 order at 09:35 says about the rest of the day

Overnight net drift predicts nothing (ρ +0.04 with rest-of-day). But the EMA ordering at the bell — which reads the overnight's shape, not its net — carries a long-side lean: bull-stacked opens beat non-bull opens in every overnight tercile, and the strongest cell is a flat overnight that still built a bull stack. The bear stack carries nothing. Four real days below, picked near their cell medians (B is the deliberate exception: the cell's worst loss).
