Now do it for every day and look at the shape

One session tells you nothing. Compute the ratio for the whole sample, plot the histogram, and read the silhouette. This is the entire test — and it decides whether the idea is worth spending money on.

Two things that will bite

Drift. Volume and volatility both wander over the years, so a raw ratio partly just tracks “was this a busy month.” Compare each day against its own trailing 20-session median instead of against the whole sample — otherwise the two humps found are 2024 and 2026.

The sharper version. Range over total volume is the readable form. The precise form uses signed flow: net buy-initiated minus sell-initiated volume against signed price change. That is Kyle’s lambda, and it needs the aggressor tags — where B is a buy aggressor and A a sell. Check interactions.py:266 before trusting the output; the sign flip there would invert this study silently.
