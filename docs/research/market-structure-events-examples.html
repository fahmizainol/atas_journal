BOS / CHoCH — market structure as data, on the chart

Companion to market-structure-events.md. The purple line is the non-repainting causal zigzag; dots are confirmed swing pivots labelled HH/HL/LH/LL. Dashed verticals are break events with the broken swing drawn as a short horizontal tick and the forward-20-bar net beside them. The thin strip along the top is the bias state per bar (blue = up, red = down). Everything shown is a column in structure_events.parquet — no pixels were needed to compute it.

1 · Reading the labels — the opening up-staircase NQH5 2025-02-03 · 09:30–09:46 ET · 10pt swings
READ
Purely didactic: confirmed swings step up, each higher high tagged HH over a higher low HL, and every close above the prior swing high fires a BOS↑ (continuation). The purple line and the four two-letter labels ARE market structure, as columns. No outcome claim here — panels 2–4 are where the money question lives.
2 · The null as whipsaw — three character-flips in ~15 minutes NQH5 2025-02-03 · 14:58–15:20 ET · 5pt swings
LOSS
At a 5pt swing threshold the bias flips up→down→up three times, each CHoCH worth a handful of points forward. Structure is being redefined almost every bar; “change of character” here is just two-sided rotation. This is what a ~49.5% win rate looks like on a chart.
3 · The dream — a CHoCH that marked a real turn NQM5 2025-04-09 · 10:28–11:02 ET · 10pt swings
WIN
An uptrend's higher-low is knifed: CHoCH↓, bias flips down, and price falls for the next 20 bars almost without a pullback (MAE ~1pt). This is the trade the SMC vocabulary promises. Keep panel 4 in view before you believe it.
4 · The trap — the SAME CHoCH signal, inverted outcome NQM5 2025-04-07 · 09:55–10:24 ET · 10pt swings
LOSS
Identical setup two days earlier: a CHoCH↓ fires… and price rips the other way for hundreds of points against the flip. Panels 3 and 4 are the whole study: the same event precedes the best and the worst outcomes, so pooled it nets ~zero. The label is real; the edge is not.

Panels 3 and 4 use different sessions but the identical rule; the swing threshold is set to 10pt for legibility (5pt in panel 2 to show the noise floor). The forward-outcome null holds at every threshold — see §4 of the write-up.
