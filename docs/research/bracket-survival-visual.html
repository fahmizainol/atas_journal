What this is and is not

Entries are drawn at random through the session at the pace the behaviour audit measured, and the side is a coin flip. So none of this is a claim that a bracket makes money — at zero edge the mean day is costs, and it shows. What it does measure is the part of survival that belongs to the geometry: how a stop distance, a target and a trail interact with a drawdown floor when the entries are worth nothing. A shape that holds up here holds up because of its shape.

Days are resampled with replacement, so serial correlation between real sessions is gone by construction — these are the odds over an i.i.d. day book, which is the same frame lucidpro-50k-survivability.md used.
