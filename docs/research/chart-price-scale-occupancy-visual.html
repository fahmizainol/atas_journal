The candles are using a quarter of the pane

Four NQ sessions, same pane (563 css px @ DPR 2), measured in the browser. The price scale is held open by context layers anchored hundreds of points from price — so the traded range gets compressed into a ribbon. Recovering that space is what makes ticks look smooth; it isn't a renderer problem.

  • The ±1σ/±2σ VWAP lines are the largest single offender every day. mkBand is called four times and its line() helper sets no autoscaleInfoProvider, so all 20 lines vote. Demoting the 16 non-mid ones is the same call :1884 already makes for Modern VWAP.
  • The weekly VWAP mid is the decisive second. On 08-05 it alone takes the scale 1078 → 260 pts. Its mid sat near 29,050 with price at 29,915 — on an anchor the code itself calls context, nothing the replay measures against.
  • The anchored mid costs nothing here — the ⚓ band is built empty and has no points to fit until someone anchors.
  • The composite VP levels do not vote at all. They're drawn inside CompositeProfilePrimitive, and no primitive on this chart implements autoscaleInfo. The C-VAL 29032.50 label sitting near the weekly mid on 08-05 is a coincidence — it was the first suspect, and demoting layers one at a time refuted it.

The trade-off

A level that stops voting still draws at its true price — it just gets clipped instead of squashing everything else. On these days that means the composite VAL and the weekly band leave the screen until price comes near them. “Off-screen” and “doesn't exist” look identical, so the honest version of this change ships with an edge marker naming the nearest off-screen level and its distance.
