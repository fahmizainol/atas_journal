What you are looking at

Both accounts carry the same Maximum Loss Limit — $1,000 on the 25K, $2,000 on the 50K — and the same lock: once the balance clears the initial trail balance, the floor freezes at start + $100 and never moves again.

The only difference is when the floor is allowed to move. LucidPro steps it once, at the daily close. LucidDaily tracks your running peak — including money that is only open profit. That gap is the tax in the ledger: the deepest drop below your running peak, minus the deepest drop below where the day started. It is exactly the profit you touched and gave back, and it is zero on a day you were never green.

Trades here are synthetic: an outcome is drawn against the win-rate slider, then a random path is generated to it, so an open position wanders before it resolves. That wandering is the whole point — a floor that only watched closed balances would never see it. Commission is charged as a round turn at entry ($3.50/side on NQ, $0.50/side per micro), matching data/research/replay-trail/intraday_dd.py.
