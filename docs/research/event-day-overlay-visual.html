Event-Day Overlay: macro releases, earnings, and strategy performance

Do FOMC, CPI, NFP, PCE, GDP, or NQ-heavyweight earnings days move the sim strategies? Answer: no — and the null survived an out-of-sample test.

2026-07-19 window 2025-02-03 → 2026-06-30 upper-band baseline v10 · 398 trades source: docs/research/event-day-overlay.md

Verdict: no event gate, no event sizing, no stay-flat veto. Event days show no significant effect on any strategy (p = 0.58), the first-pass in-sample leads reversed when three fresh months of data arrived, and post-earnings sessions are — if anything — slightly better than baseline. Backlog item 1 is closed; the reusable artifact is the verified release calendar at the bottom.

TL;DRFive findings, one decision

  • No macro event-day effect. Upper-band-bounce v10: +0.091 R/trade on event days vs +0.168 elsewhere — permutation p = 0.579. Win rate barely moves (69.2% vs 70.6%).
  • The in-sample leads died out of sample. On the v8 window NFP looked soft (−0.032) and event+post-day softness reached p = 0.088. Adding Mar–Jun 2026: NFP flipped to +0.154, and event+post washed out to p = 0.304. refuted OOS
  • No earnings damage. All six heavyweights report after the close, so the affected session is the next day — and those sessions run +0.208 R/trade at 76.7% win, slightly better than baseline.
  • Pre-event days are fine (+0.116, 75% win). There is no pre-event-chop story.
  • FOMC exposure barely exists. Across 17 months the profitable strategies took 3 FOMC-day trades — all entered before the 14:00 statement. Only the losing strategies ever traded after it.

§1Method

Each session date is tagged with any of {FOMC decision, CPI, NFP, PCE, GDP} from the verified calendar (§5); roughly 22% of trading days carry at least one tag. pre is the last traded day before an event day, post the first after, between both at once, clean none. Earnings tags: every report in the window was after-market-close, so post-earnings marks the next traded session and report-day the RTH session before the print. Tags join per-trade on session; significance is permutation-tested (20k shuffles, two-sided) on mean R/trade. The thin October-2025-only strategies are excluded — four to nine days each, no power.

FOMC statement-time alignment

Upper-band and profile-pullback have zero entries after 14:00 ET on FOMC days. Post-statement trading exists only in value-rotation (+0.36, 12 trades) and dev1-fade (−0.37, 13). The one event that fires mid-session is one the profitable strategies structurally never trade into.

§3Why the null is convincing

  • A built-in out-of-sample test. The first pass ran on the v8 baseline (window ending 2026-02-20) and produced three leads: NFP soft, GDP soft, event+post softness at p = 0.088. The baseline then moved to v10, whose Mar–Jun 2026 tail was unseen when those hypotheses formed. The fresh data flipped NFP positive, shrank everything else, and washed the composite out — the same in-sample-mirage signature as the seven A/B-failed order-flow knobs.
  • The mechanism check fails. If events were toxic, win rate should drop, or CPI — the biggest scheduled vol print — should be the worst bucket. Neither holds: win rates are flat across tags and CPI is positive in both samples.
  • Tail concentration predicts exactly this. With ~20 trades carrying ≈100% of net, a binary tag over ~22% of days shows a "large" R/trade gap by chance roughly a third of the time. Day-level tagging cannot beat that noise floor at n ≈ 400.

§4First-pass numbers, for the record (v8, 279 trades)

Pre/post cut: clean +0.291, pre +0.220, event +0.060, post +0.105, between −0.484; event+post vs rest −0.171, p = 0.088. Every negative lead regressed or reversed on the extended sample.
