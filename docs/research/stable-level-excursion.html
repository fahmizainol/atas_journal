Net position at +60m — distribution by level

25-tick bins, clipped at ±400t. Every level: wide, roughly centered, mild rightward lean = the sample's bull drift, not the level.

Read: 54% of touches finish the hour above the level, median net +20t — but the typical touch first pokes ~150t up and ~150t down. No level's net mean is distinguishable from zero (all |t|<1.4), and the continue-through vs bounce-back ratio is 0.90–1.03 everywhere: price travels equally far in both directions off every level. The only per-level fingerprint is swing size: VAL touches carry the widest envelopes (~176–190t) — they happen in faster tape — vs ~130–150t at POC/VAH. Level identity says how violent the next hour is, not which way it goes. The apparent VAH-as-support / VAL-as-resistance leans are session drift: on those same sessions, other touches moved further in the same direction (e.g. ny_vah/support +34t vs +43t for its own sessions' other touches) — the cells lag their regime, they don't lead it.
